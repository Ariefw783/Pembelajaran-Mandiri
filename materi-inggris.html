Materi: Possessive Adjectives (His/Her)

Ringkasan Materi

1. HIS (Milik Dia Laki-laki)

Digunakan untuk laki-laki (He).

Example: He is Adi. He is his brother.

2. HER (Milik Dia Perempuan)

Digunakan untuk perempuan (She).

Example: She is Ani. She is her sister.

Father = Ayah | Mother = Ibu | Brother = Saudara Laki-laki | Sister = Saudara Perempuan

A. PerempuanB. Laki-lakiC. Benda
